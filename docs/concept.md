# Concept

## In one paragraph

Ground State is a blockchain seen as a place. The map is not drawn by anyone: it is the address
space itself — all 2<sup>160</sup> addresses — and every address has exactly one location. Everything
that stands in the world is real and obeys the rules of the chain: contracts stand as buildings,
their shape from the hash of their code, their size from its length, their darkness from their
balance; wallets lie as plates with a post for every token they hold, the name and the amount cut
into the post in signs; and the current block crosses the sky in real time, every transaction from
its sender towards its receiver, the reverted ones wavering and withdrawn. People walk here
together. Empty ground is taken by work, not money, and what is built on it is a real contract
living at the address of that place.

Ethereum today; a way of seeing a chain, not one chain.

## Why stand in it

1. **Another way of seeing a chain.** Not a table and not an explorer: a place, and everything in
   it by the chain's own rules — what the chain says is what stands. Walk to your wallet and see
   your tokens; walk to USDC and watch transactions come down on it. No wallet, no install.
2. **Ground that cannot be bought.** A plot is a contract whose address is fixed before it exists
   (CREATE2), so mining a salt is mining a location: the longer you dig, the closer. There is no
   auction and nobody sells land. The price is your processor and your time. Nowhere else offers
   this.
3. **Stake a place and build on it.** One transaction and the place is your contract, forever.
   Then write into it, name it under `groundstate.eth`, point it at any contract of your own — a
   casino, a gallery, a game — and that code runs at this very address; pass it on, sell it, rent
   it, seal its code for good. Plots stand beside plots, and streets and towns come out of it. The
   relics of the world's earlier versions stand among them with the signs of their age.

## What it does today

**See.** Two chains: Ethereum to walk, Sepolia to take ground on. Arrive at an address or an ENS
name. Buildings from code, plates from wallets with runes for their tokens, the block's traffic
overhead. A 2D map of the whole address space.

**Walk.** Keyboard and mouse; a thumb stick on a phone. Other people are seen, their digging is
seen, their claims go up on every screen within seconds.

**Take.** Digging on every core in WebAssembly (about seven million attempts a second a thread),
a slider for the cores, paused while the tab is hidden, what is found kept in the browser. One
transaction claims it, and the plot is drawn as a blueprint where it landed.

**Build.** Write a line into a plot, name it `x.groundstate.eth`, point it at your contract, seal
the code, hand the plot to somebody else.

**Remember.** A subgraph knows every plot, its owner, what is written into it, its name. The
relics of the first two factories stand as the world's history.

## What it is not

There is no scenery, no time as a dimension, no mock data, and no content anybody at Ground State
authors. If a place is empty, the chain has nothing there. If a place looks like something, the
chain says so.
